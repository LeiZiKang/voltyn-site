Features

From this second to the whole week.

Three places, three jobs: the menu bar for now, the widget for the last hour, and Details for longer stretches.

01 · Menu bar

Glance up. Know right now.

Open the menu bar panel to see current system power and a 60-second live chart. Power source, energy flow, battery health and temperature all live in the same panel.

  • System power with a 60-second live chart
  • Input → System → Battery power flow
  • Estimated battery health, temperature and cycles
  • Adapter capability and battery power

02 · Desktop widget

Keep the trend on your desktop.

The medium widget shows the recorded one-hour mean and trend. Looking at a stretch of time makes changes in energy use far easier to spot than chasing every spike.

  • One-hour mean with trend bars
  • Clearly labelled snapshot time
  • Visible without opening the app

The widget shows a snapshot, not a continuously refreshing gauge. Read it together with its update time.

03 · Details history

Zoom out and spot the pattern.

Switch between 60 seconds, 1 hour, 1 day and 7 days in the Details window to review the power data Voltyn actually recorded, with recorded mean, peak and deeper battery readings.

  • 60 s / 1 h / 1 d / 7 d ranges
  • Recorded mean and peak
  • Battery voltage, current and full-charge capacity
  • Unrecorded periods stay as gaps, never filled with zero

Readings

Not every watt means the same thing.

Charger wattage, charging input and what your Mac is consuming are easy to mix up. Voltyn shows them separately and labels each one.

01System powerWhat your Mac uses
Overall power your Mac is using right now. The menu bar shows the live reading; the widget shows a one-hour mean.
02Input powerWhat comes in
Power currently read on the supply input side. When macOS offers no usable value, Voltyn never substitutes adapter capability or zero.
03Adapter capabilityWhat the charger can offer
The maximum your charger reports it can deliver, such as 100 W. It is not what your Mac is using or charging at right now.

Local first

Your power history stays on your Mac.

The power history Voltyn records is stored locally, with no automatic telemetry and no account. Content only leaves your device when you choose to share a screenshot or feedback.

FAQ

Before you buy.

Full support guide (Chinese)
Which Macs does Voltyn support?

MacBook models with Apple silicon (M-series chips) running macOS 14 or later. Intel Macs and desktop Macs are not supported.

Is it a subscription?

No. Voltyn is a one-time purchase on the Mac App Store with no subscription and no in-app purchases. The price shown on the App Store for your region applies.

My charger says 100 W. Why is the reading lower?

Adapter capability is the most your charger can deliver, system power is what your Mac is using, and input power is the current reading on the supply side. A lower reading does not mean your charger or cable is faulty.

Why do the widget and menu bar show different numbers?

The menu bar shows live system power, while the medium widget shows a recorded one-hour mean tied to its snapshot time. Different time scales usually mean different numbers.

Does Voltyn upload my data?

Not automatically. Recorded power history stays on your Mac with no automatic telemetry. Content only leaves your device when you send an email, screenshot or feedback yourself.
